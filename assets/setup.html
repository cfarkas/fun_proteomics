Assign and name your samples

Drag detected samples into a condition, or use the dropdown on each card. Drag between columns to correct an assignment, or back to Unassigned to exclude a sample. Edit sample and condition names as needed.

Only assigned samples enter the matrices, benchmarks and comparisons. Conditions are never inferred from filenames. Source filenames and columns remain linked when you rename a sample.
